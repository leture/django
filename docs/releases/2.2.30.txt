===========================
Django 2.2.30 release notes
===========================

*October 4, 2022*

Django 2.2.30 fixes one security issues with severity "medium" in 2.2.29.

CVE-2022-41323: Potential denial-of-service vulnerability in internationalized URLs
===================================================================================

Internationalized URLs were subject to potential denial of service attack via
the locale parameter.

Fix was backported from git, https://github.com/django/django/commit/5b6b257fa7ec37ff27965358800c67e2dd11c924.
